Research

May 2026 – presentQMIND Lab, UCLA

Predicting Tc in kagome superconductors

Exploring machine learning and AI methods to understand how defects influence the critical temperature of kagome superconductors. Developing agentic pipelines that use RAG grounded in scientific literature to generate physics-informed features and regression models, which are then validated against experimental data. The goal is to use AI agents to improve and potentially automate scientific modeling while gaining deeper insight into the mechanisms underlying superconductivity in the kagome lattice.
